# Statistical appendix

All summaries use the training seed as the independent unit (n=3). Values are reported as mean ± sample SD. Exploratory t intervals are stored in summary.csv, but with two degrees of freedom they are too wide for strong inference.

No significance test is reported: with n=3, normality tests are not informative and the minimum attainable two-sided exact Wilcoxon p-value cannot establish p<0.05. Episode-level pooling would be pseudoreplication.

This analysis establishes optimization stability only. A method comparison requires the fixed-mix standard PPO runs with identical training seeds and budget.
