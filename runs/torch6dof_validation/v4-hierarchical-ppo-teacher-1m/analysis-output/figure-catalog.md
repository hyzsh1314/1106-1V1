# Figure catalog

## Figure 1 — Fixed-opponent performance

Purpose: show task outcome and weapon-opportunity quality by opponent. Points are seeds; diamonds and error bars are mean ± sample SD (n=3). Notice the stable easy-opponent performance and persistent evasive timeout bottleneck.

## Figure 2 — Gate training dynamics

Purpose: verify that option usage and teacher-target accuracy remain stable throughout optimization. No smoothing is applied. This supports removal of collapse, but not teacher-free learning.

## Figure 3 — Option specialization

Purpose: show the learned division of labor. Bars are mean ± sample SD (n=3). The gate uses base for hold/pursuit and intercept for evasive; remaining evasive failures therefore mostly arise below the gate.
